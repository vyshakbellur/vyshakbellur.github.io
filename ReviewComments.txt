My One-Sentence Recommendation

Don't evolve this site into a bigger portfolio. Evolve it into a living record of influence.

Every future milestone—an invited keynote, a published paper, a judging role, media coverage, an award, a successful research collaboration—should appear here in a way that is independently verifiable and easy to understand.

One final observation

You mentioned that this site is meant to attract speaking invitations, judging opportunities, publishers, and eventually support an EB1A narrative. Looking at it as a whole, I think your instinct was right to avoid a conventional portfolio. A memorable visual identity gives people a reason to stay.

The next stage isn't adding more personality—it's making your impact impossible to miss.

If, over the next few years, you continue publishing quality research, writing thoughtful technical essays, speaking at conferences, judging competitions, and documenting those accomplishments here with the same level of craftsmanship you've already shown in the design, this website can become a genuine professional asset rather than just a personal homepage. That's a much stronger long-term strategy than chasing visual trends or repeatedly redesigning the site.
